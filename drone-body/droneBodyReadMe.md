For the mechanical design of this drone, my primary philosophy was to keep everything as simple and practical as possible. 
I intentionally avoided putting any cutouts or mounting holes in the center of the body. 
Even though this solid core would make the frame slightly heavier, 
I prioritized making the structure as strong as possible to survive inevitable crashes during testing. 
The main challenge was finding the balance between structural strength and maintaining a good thrust-to-weight ratio. 
To compensate for the heavier solid core, I decided to avoid complex design, 
dedicated mounting spaces for the ESP32, battery and other electronics.
Instead, I chose to secure all the components simply using tape. 
This decision reduced the overall weight of the frame and kept the CAD design extremely straightforward.
In this Project avoiding over-engineering in the mechanical phase helped me increase my productivity a lot, 
allowing me to move quickly from 3D printing directly to flight testing.
